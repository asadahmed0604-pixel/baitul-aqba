Donor portal

  • Pay for one month or several months in advance
  • Upload the receipt; the details are read automatically
  • Track the status of every entry

Foundation management

Review, verify and report on all donations.

  • Weekly and monthly reports
  • Month-by-month coverage for every orphan
  • Import and export data (Excel / CSV)
